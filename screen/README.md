# I.A.M. screen (graduation show)

Open `/screen/` in Chrome, choose the camera, press Start. A passerby gets a material head with eye and mouth holes; closing a hand beside the head squeezes it the way that material responds and floats up one imagined voice from the public archive (`entries.voice`, read only).

* Face and hand tracking: MediaPipe Tasks Vision 0.10.14 (Apache 2.0), bundled in `lib/` and `models/`, runs locally.
* Offline: after one visit a service worker keeps every file; voices are cached in the browser.
* Settings and moderation (blocklist, hidden ids, approved only): `config.js`.
* Keys: F full screen, C camera, D debug, S test squeeze, M mirror.
* Head images are reference cut outs, not specimen photographs. Shaving Foam, Rose and Cabbage Leaf have none yet.
